## 3. Customer support
- **Channels:** in-app help centre (FAQs in EN and Pidgin), in-app ticket form (attaches app version, device and user ID automatically), email `help@whosabi.ng`, and an X DM watch.
- **Tooling:** a helpdesk (Freshdesk or Zendesk free tier, or Crisp), linked to User 360 in the admin console, so the agent sees the player's timeline, sessions, matches and purchases next to the ticket.
- **SLAs:** first response within 24 h (P1 issues like a lost purchase within 4 h in working hours); resolution within 72 h.
- **Top macros:** lost streak, purchase not received (check RevenueCat → re-grant), wrong question dispute, username change, account deletion, banned appeal, OTP not received (check the provider logs per carrier).
- **Built in step 2.7p:** the app's Help screen (Settings → Help and support) has the common questions in English and Pidgin and a form that sends the topic, the message, an optional email, and the app's version and the device. Each message is kept for the console (**Support**, and User 360's Support tab) and emailed to `SUPPORT_INBOX`, the helpdesk's inbox, with the player's email to reply to and a link to their page. Support answers by email from the helpdesk, or in the console, where the answer shows in the player's Help screen; the ready answers above are in the console in both languages. Whichever helpdesk is chosen (Freshdesk, Zendesk or Crisp), it takes these by email: point `help@whosabi.ng` at it and set `SUPPORT_INBOX`.
- **Compensation policy:** the support role can grant up to 500 coins a day per player. Larger grants are made by an admin, and anything above 5,000 needs a second approver (doc 18 §8). Every grant goes through the ledger (`reason='support_grant'`).

## 4. Moderation
- **Usernames & nicknames:** automatic filter at creation plus user reports; moderators can force a rename.
- **Question Factory submissions:** community votes, then editor approval; a rejected submission sends a friendly reason.
- **Prohibited:** tribal or religious insults, sexual content, hate speech, doxxing, impersonating celebrities (verified-badge system for real creators in v2).
- **Integrity flags** are reviewed daily in the admin console, highest risk first. Nothing bans a player automatically.

## 5. On-call & runbooks
- One engineer on call each week with a backup; paging through Opsgenie or PagerDuty (free tiers) for SEV1/SEV2.
- **Runbooks (to write during S6):**
  1. OTP failure on one carrier → switch the provider route.
  2. Realtime node unhealthy → drain and replace.
  3. DB failover.
  4. Bad question live → suspend + re-score.
  5. Ads causing crashes → disable the network or format through a flag.
  6. Push storm or duplicate pushes → pause the queue.
  7. Suspected breach → incident process (doc 10 §7).
  8. Daily not generated by 23:30 → manual publish.
  9. Event ingest or ClickHouse down → confirm gameplay is unaffected, watch the stream backlog (it holds several hours before the length cap drops the oldest events), restore, let the loader catch up, and show "data delayed" in the console.
  10. Broken web app release → point CloudFront back at the previous release folder.
  11. Suspected staff misuse of player data → suspend the admin account, export its audit trail, start the incident process with the DPO.

## 6. Status & comms
- In-app and web status banner controlled from the admin console ("Party rooms are having issues. Duels still work").
- An X account for status updates. Post-mortems summarised publicly for major incidents.
